Our story & purpose

Connecting people with
the skills they need.

Work Satthi is a hyperlocal platform built to make everyday services easier to find, fairer to arrange, and more accessible to everyone — customers and workers alike.

The problem we address

Finding everyday help shouldn't be hard.

Millions of people across India need help with routine tasks — a leaking tap, a broken switch, a home that needs cleaning. At the same time, millions of skilled workers struggle to find steady work beyond word-of-mouth recommendations.

These two groups live in the same neighbourhoods, yet rarely connect in a structured, transparent, or fair way. Work Satthi exists to change that.

What we do

A platform for both sides of local work.

Work Satthi gives customers a clear, accessible way to describe what they need, browse available workers, agree on fair pricing, and request a booking.

For service professionals, the platform provides a free way to list their skills, share their service area and rates, and become discoverable to nearby customers — without relying solely on referrals.

All communication is meant to be transparent: prices should be discussed before work begins, and no booking is confirmed until availability is verified.

Our principles

What we stand for,
every step of the way.

These are not aspirational slogans. They are the practical commitments that shape every decision in how the platform is built and operated.

01 · Access

Make services easy to find

Anyone with a phone and a need should be able to discover relevant local help without barriers, jargon, or complexity.

02 · Fairness

Support fair pricing

Prices are discussed openly between customers and workers before work begins. We do not set or control rates — that power stays with the people.

03 · Dignity

Respect for workers

Service professionals deserve to have their skills recognised, their time valued, and their contributions respected in every interaction.

04 · Trust

Transparent communication

We encourage honest, clear communication between customers and workers. No surprises, no hidden information, no fake guarantees.

05 · Safety

Safety-focused by design

The platform is built to encourage safer interactions through clear agreements, open profiles, and honest booking processes.

06 · Community

Local economic growth

When local workers find more work, and local customers get reliable help, whole communities become stronger and more self-sufficient.

Where we are now

An early-stage platform,
built with care.

Work Satthi is at an early stage of development. The frontend platform is live and the backend API is ready for deployment. Authentication, booking, and worker registration will be fully functional once the API is deployed and connected.

We are not making claims about verified workers, guaranteed response times, or confirmed bookings. What we are building is a foundation for those capabilities to exist in a trustworthy way.
